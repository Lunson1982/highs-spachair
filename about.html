About HIGHS

We Make Spa Chairs.
We Make Them Right.

Since 1999, HIGHS has been designing and manufacturing professional multi-functional spa chairs for beauty salons, nail bars, and spas worldwide.

Our Story

26 Years. One Product Category. Done Exceptionally Well.

HIGHS was founded with a simple belief: beauty salons deserve chairs that are built as beautifully as the services they provide. Not repurposed furniture. Not generic salon equipment. Purpose-engineering, commercially-rated spa chairs.

Today, HIGHS chairs are found in 5,000+ salons across 50+ countries — from boutique nail bars in Vancouver to high-volume spa chains in Dubai and Manila. Every model is the result of direct feedback from salon owners and technicians who use our chairs every day.

We don't make furniture. We make spa chairs. That's all we do. And we think that focus shows.
